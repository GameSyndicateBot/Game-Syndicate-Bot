# GS CORE V18.3.15 — Role Lock, 5-Minute Registration, @everyone

- Registration for World Boss shortened from 10 minutes to 5 minutes.
- Boss spawn message now pings `@everyone` when the bot has permission to mention everyone.
- Required raid composition:
  - 4–5 players: exactly 1 tank and 1 healer.
  - 6–9 players: exactly 1 tank and 2 healers.
  - 10+ players: exactly 2 tanks and 2–3 healers.
- Tank/healer overfill is blocked according to the group size.
- When the remaining class picks are needed to fill missing tank/healer minimums, late pickers can only choose those missing roles.
- Automatic class selection obeys the same restrictions.
