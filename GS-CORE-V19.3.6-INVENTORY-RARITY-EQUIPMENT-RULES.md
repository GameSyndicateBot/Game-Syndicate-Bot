# GS CORE V19.3.6 — Inventory rarity and equipment rules

- Inventory rarity spheres: Common white, Rare blue, Epic purple, Legendary orange, Mythic red.
- Equipped items use their rarity sphere in both active slots and the item list; equipped status is written as text.
- Active mount is shown as a separate inventory slot.
- Mounts are no longer mixed into the pet activation page.
- Class-based weapon restrictions remain enabled.
- Two-handed weapons cannot be equipped together with a shield.
- Companion and mount sale/exchange integration from V19.3.5 is preserved.
