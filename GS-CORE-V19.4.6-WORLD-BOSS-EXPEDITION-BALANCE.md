# GS Core V19.4.6 — World Boss & Expedition Balance

- Exact flat HP from equipped items, active pets and active mount is applied in World Boss.
- All active companions and mount bonuses are included in hero snapshots.
- Mirror Lord rage ultimate uses a fixed total damage budget instead of multiplying card damage per player.
- Boss rage gain and phase multipliers reduced; rage ult has a 4-round cooldown.
- Iron Colossus overheats much less often and is stronger on normal turns.
- Eye of the Abyss, Chain Lord, Mother of Dragons and Time Keeper damage/control/summon cadence normalized.
- Expedition chance is calculated once at launch, shown to the player, saved, and used at resolution.
- Equipment, pets, mount, alchemy and world effects are included in that saved chance.
