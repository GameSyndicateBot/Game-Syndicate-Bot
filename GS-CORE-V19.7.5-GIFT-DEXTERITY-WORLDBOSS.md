# GS CORE V19.7.5 — PRIVATE GIFTS & DPS DEXTERITY

- Gift flow remains fully ephemeral and acknowledges recipient selection immediately.
- DPS classes now receive extra World Boss damage from Dexterity on equipped items.
- Formula: every 2 equipped Dexterity = +1% outgoing damage, capped at +30%.
- Base hero Dexterity, companions, mounts and temporary effects are excluded from this dedicated bonus.
- The bonus applies through the shared outgoing-damage multiplier to normal attacks, skills and ultimates.
